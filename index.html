The name is short for hypercube.

Extend a point and you get a line. Extend the line and you get a square, then a cube. Extend the cube one more time and you get a shape that is hard to picture and easy to reason about: a hypercube, one dimension up from the space you are standing in. From there, everything below it is clear.

That is the move we make on every project. Step one level above the product, the team, or the routine, look at the whole system, and come back with something new and useful: a table, a bus route, a way to teach forgiveness.
